Sidenote Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

Sidenote is a browser extension: you select a word or sentence on a web page and get a short explanation from an AI model of your choice. This page explains what data that involves and where it goes.

In short

  • Sidenote has no server. The developer receives none of your data.
  • When you ask for an explanation, the text needed for it is sent directly from your browser to the AI service you configured, using your API key.
  • Your notes, settings and API keys are stored only in your browser.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no sale of data.

What is sent, and when

Nothing is sent until you act: clicking Explain, Example or Ask, pressing one of Sidenote's shortcuts, sending a follow-up question, or clicking "Test connection" in Settings. Then Sidenote sends:

  • the text you selected;
  • the passage around it (up to about 1,500 characters) and the section headings above it;
  • the page title, and on chat sites (claude.ai, ChatGPT) the question you asked there;
  • for follow-ups, your question and the earlier answers in that note;
  • for a note opened inside another note, the original passage and the chain of terms you selected.

It is sent over HTTPS to the service selected in Settings (Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Zhipu, SiliconFlow, Alibaba Cloud, a local Ollama, or an endpoint you entered). If automatic fallback is on and that service fails, the same request goes to the next service you have set up with a key. What each service does with the data is governed by its own terms and privacy policy. Some free tiers (for example Google AI Studio's) may use requests to improve their models; check the provider's terms if that matters to you.

Sidenote does not read or send anything from pages where you have not enabled it, and does not send your browsing history.

What is stored on your device

Data Where
Settings and API keys chrome.storage.local: this browser profile only, not synced, never given to web pages.
Notes: the page address and title, the selected text, the surrounding passage, and the answers The extension's own IndexedDB database, so notes and highlights are there when you come back.

You can delete notes one by one in the in-page notes list or the Notebook. Removing the extension deletes all of it. An Anki export is a file you download; Sidenote does not keep a copy elsewhere.

Permissions

Permission Why
Access to websites you enable (optional) To show the Explain button on selected text and highlight explained words. Off everywhere until you enable a site or all sites. You can remove access in Settings or from Chrome's extension menu.
Access to the AI services' API addresses To send your requests to the service you chose.
storage To keep your settings and API keys.
scripting To start Sidenote on exactly the sites you enabled.
activeTab So the toolbar button can show whether Sidenote is on for the site you are viewing.

Sidenote uses Chrome's built-in, on-device language detection to tell the model which language to answer in. It does not load or run code from the internet.

Children

Sidenote is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
